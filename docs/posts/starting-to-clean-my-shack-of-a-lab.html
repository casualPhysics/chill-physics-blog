Starting to Clean My Shack Of A Lab

31 August 2026 · Cleaning ASMR for you guys.

On Sunday I spent 5 hours cleaning up the lab in my apartment. I really try to avoid organisational cleaning as much as possible. To be clear, this is not because I don't enjoy cleaning. I avoid it because I try not to get sucked into productivity-pornesque organisational tasks. Organising stuff actually feels too good, and I get carried away. And that distracts me from doing actual work like experimenting or doing math!

And so I put off doing any cleaning of the lab space for months. Even when guests came over I would leave all the lab equipment out hoping that no one would notice. It got to a point where I was just dumping stuff that I got onto my lab table when the amazon order arrived.

And that would usually be fine! BUT until a week ago it spiralled into me not being able to do experiments because I didn't have enough space. Also, it would take me much longer than usual to do experiments because I couldn't find things, since there was so much crap lying around. There was one time that I spent an hour trying to find my digital caliper, which is time pissed down the drain. This was the final red line, and so I finally caved in an decided to do some cleaning.

Here are some quotes from family about the state of the lab

'The state of your apartment makes me have a lump in my throat' - sister.

'How do you live like this' - parents.

'We need to get you your own room for your hobbies’ - my gf.

This is what the lab looked like. There is literally no space to put stuff on the table or for me to sit with a chair. The monitor takes up quite a bit of space, the HEPA laminar flow hood also takes up a significant portion of the desk, and thus I have no place to put my microscope. And I use my microscope a lot so it's a shame that it doesn't have a permanent home. Right now its on a shelf on my bookshelf.

Here's what the whole space looks like - ignore my drying laundry

Even the contents of my shelves were messy

Also the 5GBP plastering board that I put everything on looks like it's about to break. You can even see the bending in the middle. And to fix this problem I put two of my bedside tables underneath it with some books to give some structural integrity.

The first step was to get a desk. So I got ordered 2m long sturdy looking one from IKEA. This doesn't arrive till next week, but I still went ahead and started pre-organising stuff.

I have a metal cabinet shelf. I wanted to put a system to label things in these shelved properly. I also wanted to digitise the contents of what I had in the lab so that an LLM would be able to instantly tell me which experiments were possible or not. And so use this opportunity not just to clean but to do also some logging of all the stuff I had. So for your interest, here's a partial tour of what I have so far

I started to collect the easy stuff first, like glassware, and put them all in a single shelf. So I put the following in this shelf

  • test tubes

  • victorian style sterilisation lamp

  • glass petri dishes

  • assorted beakers

  • glass stirrer

Then I put all of my probes into another shelf

  • pressure manometer for biosecurity experiments

  • temperature, CO2, and particle concentration monitor for biosecurity experiments

  • ammeter / voltmenter / multimeter

  • cooking temperature probes

And then I put everything to do with electrical and circuitry in another shelf. Here I keep

  • small electronic sensors, like ultrasound sensors

  • breadboards

  • cables

  • transistors, amps, resistors

  • power adapters

  • other assorted chips like ESP32 dev board, arduino nano, etc

I put the things that can control temperature in another shelf

  • magnetic heater and stirrer - very useful for a range of things that I need to do - highly recommend as a piece of equipment

  • candles

  • matches

  • and randomly my jewellers' scale

And then safety stuff like

  • nitrile gloves

  • safety goggles in another shelf

I am thinking to have another cupboard for live samples, samples and reagants. For example I have chemicals like

  • magnesium chloride

  • silver nitrate

  • dilute hydrogen peroxide

  • caustic soda

  • agar jelly

  • silicone oil

Along with minerals for my curiosity in origins of life

  • periodite

  • olivine

  • obisidan / volcanic glass

Anyways, the lab is a work in progress, I'll keep you guys updated with the full cleanup once my shelves arrive.
